Cosmose AI (Cosmose Inc.) is a Singapore-headquartered, Polish-founded retail intelligence company that measures,
predicts and influences offline shopping behaviour, claiming reach across roughly one billion consumers through an
SDK embedded in partner mobile applications. Founded in 2014 by Miron Mironiuk. Customers include LVMH, Richemont,
L'Oreal and Estee Lauder. Two consumer products: Cosmose Media (advertising) and KaiKai / KaiKaiNow (a gamified
"shoppertainment" and lock-screen app built with the NEAR Foundation).

## What is and is not callable

Cosmose AI runs no public developer program. There is no developer portal, no API reference site, no pricing page,
no self-serve signup and no key-issuance flow. The analytics and ad-targeting product it actually sells has no
public machine-readable contract at all.

One API surface is nonetheless public: a springdoc-generated OpenAPI 3.0.1 document for the KaiKai Deal Hunter
registration service, served anonymously from the Singapore gateway.
